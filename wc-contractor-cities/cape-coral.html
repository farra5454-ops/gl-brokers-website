Workers' Compensation Insurance in Cape Coral, FL

Cape Coral has been one of Florida's fastest-growing cities for years, built on a footprint of more than 400 miles of canals and a steady pipeline of new-home construction. That growth means a constant supply of framing, roofing, concrete, electrical, and plumbing crews putting up new houses — and under Florida Statute 440, a construction business here needs workers' compensation coverage the moment it hires its first employee, no exceptions for how new the company is.

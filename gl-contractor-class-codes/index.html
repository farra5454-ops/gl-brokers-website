General Liability by Contractor Trade

General liability for contractors is rated on the trade you perform, using ISO class codes, and on an exposure basis that is normally your gross receipts or your payroll. As with workers' comp, the classification follows the work actually being done rather than what is printed on the licence — and it is the work actually being done that a carrier will look at when a claim comes in.

Florida's Construction Industry Licensing Board requires licensed contractors to carry general liability, but the practical driver is contractual. General contractors, property managers and municipalities across Lee, Collier and Charlotte counties routinely demand $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate, plus additional insured status and often a waiver of subrogation, before a crew is allowed to start.

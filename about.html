About GL Insurance Brokers

Insurance should be straightforward and accessible.

My name is Alexander Farr, and I am the owner of GL Insurance Brokers, a commercial insurance agency specializing in General Liability, Workers' Compensation, and Commercial Auto coverage for small businesses and contractors throughout Florida.

As a longtime resident of Southwest Florida, I have proudly called the Fort Myers area home for more than 30 years. I graduated from Estero High School, after which I served four years in the United States Army as a Missile Technician. Following my military service, I attended Florida Gulf Coast University and earned a degree in Business Management.

A Career Built on Commercial Insurance

For the past eight years, I have built my career in the insurance industry with a focus on commercial insurance solutions. My specialty is helping small businesses, contractors, and entrepreneurs secure the coverage they need to protect their operations, satisfy contract requirements, and grow with confidence.

Whether you're a startup, an established contractor, or a growing company, I work directly with clients to identify risks and find coverage solutions that fit their specific needs.

Integrity, Reliability & Service

At GL Insurance Brokers, I believe insurance should be straightforward and accessible. My goal is to provide fast quotes, competitive rates, and personalized service from a knowledgeable professional who understands the unique challenges business owners face.

My military background, business education, and years of industry experience have shaped my commitment to integrity, reliability, and exceptional customer service — the responsive, one-on-one support that large call centers simply cannot match.

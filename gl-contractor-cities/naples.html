Naples General Liability FAQ

Protecting high-value properties is the driver — it’s common for these communities to ask for $1,000,000 or $2,000,000 in coverage before granting a vendor or contractor access, well above what state law requires.

Not the statutory requirement, since Florida doesn’t mandate GL for most businesses — but it strongly affects the practical minimum a Naples client or community will actually accept, and can make a case for adding excess/umbrella coverage.

Landscaping, pool service, luxury remodeling, and cleaning/concierge services are the businesses most frequently asked to show proof of active GL coverage before starting work.

General Liability Insurance by City

Every contractor in Southwest Florida eventually hits the same wall: the general contractor, the property manager, or the municipality will not let a crew on site without a certificate of insurance, and often not without being named as an additional insured on the policy. Florida's Construction Industry Licensing Board sets the floor, but the real-world standard on commercial jobs across Lee, Collier and Charlotte counties runs to $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate.

What changes from city to city is the exposure underneath that certificate. A seawall crew working over a Cape Coral canal, a roofer on a Sanibel elevated build, and a retail build-out contractor in Estero are all buying “general liability,” but they are not carrying the same risk — and they should not be paying the same rate. Local permitting, flood zone, wind exposure and the mix of residential versus commercial work all move the number.

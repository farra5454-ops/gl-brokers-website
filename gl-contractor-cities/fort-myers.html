Fort Myers General Liability FAQ

Florida does not mandate general liability for most businesses outright, but it is effectively required in practice — Lee County general contractors, property managers, and municipal projects will ask for a certificate of insurance before letting you start, and licensed contractors have their own coverage obligations tied to licensure.

Because an uninsured subcontractor becomes the general contractor’s problem. If you damage property or injure someone and carry no coverage, the claim can land on the GC’s policy — so most simply will not hire a sub who cannot produce a current certificate.

$1,000,000 per occurrence with a $2,000,000 aggregate is the figure most commonly requested in this market. Larger commercial or municipal jobs sometimes ask for more, which is where an excess or umbrella policy layered on top becomes the practical answer.

What Affects a Commercial Auto Quote?

There is no single price that applies to every Fort Myers business. Insurers commonly consider a combination of factors, including:

Vehicle type, value, weight and repair costs

Business use, annual mileage and operating radius

Driver experience, records and assignment of vehicles

Claims history and requested limits

Garaging location and applicable underwriting rules

Whether vehicles carry tools, materials or cargo

Accurate information helps the insurer evaluate the risk. If a customer, general contractor or lender gave you insurance requirements, share the exact wording before selecting limits.

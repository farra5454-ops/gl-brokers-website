WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
===========================================================

Every file that needs to go to GitHub. Nothing else.

easiest method is:

  Drag the "assets" folder AND "sitemap.xml" into the
  root of your repo on GitHub. Choose to overwrite /
  replace when it asks.

===========================================================
AFTER YOU UPLOAD
===========================================================

1. Wait about a minute for GitHub Pages to rebuild.

2. Go to glinsurancebrokers.com/about and press
   CTRL + SHIFT + R.

   This is a hard refresh. A NORMAL refresh will show you
   the old cached stylesheet and you will think the upload
   failed again.

3. You should see Alex's face as a still image, next to
   the text, with a play button over it.

4. Press play. Confirm you hear him talking.

5. Narrow the browser window until the page goes to a
   single column. The video should drop below the text
   and stay full width.
